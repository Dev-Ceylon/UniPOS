# User guide (cashiers and owners)

## Signing in and switching cashier

- The shop's device stays signed in with an owner or manager account.
- Each staff member has a 4–6 digit **PIN**. Tap your name in the top bar (or **Switch cashier**) and enter your PIN; sales are recorded under your name until someone else switches in or you press **✕** next to your name.
- Five wrong PINs lock that person's PIN for 15 minutes. A manager can set a new PIN in **Settings → Staff & roles**.

## Opening the day

1. Go to **Shifts** (or press **Open shift** on the register) and enter the opening float.
2. Open **Register**.

## Selling

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Find a product | Type in the search box or scan with a USB scanner (it types and presses Enter). Press **F2** or **/** to jump to search. |
| Camera scan | Camera button (Chrome/Edge). |
| Change quantity | − / + buttons, type in the box, or **+ / −** keys for the selected line. |
| Remove a line | Bin icon or **Delete**. |
| Discount | % icon on a line, or **Discount** for the whole sale (**F7**). Large discounts need a manager. |
| Change price | Tag icon (needs permission). |
| Customer | Customer link above the cart (**F6**); search or create. |
| Hold / resume | **F8** to hold, **F9** to resume. |
| Pay | **F4** or **Pay**. Add one or more tenders: cash (quick-cash buttons), card (record the reference from the terminal), wallet, gift card, points. Change due is shown in green. |
| After payment | **Enter** starts a new sale; **Print receipt** prints an 80 mm receipt; **Email receipt** sends it. |

Press **?** on the register for the shortcut list.

### Offline

If the internet drops, a yellow bar says *Offline*. Keep selling: sales are saved on the device and the counter in the top bar shows how many are waiting. They sync automatically when the connection returns (or tap the counter to sync now). Creating customers, checking gift card balances and switching cashier need a connection.

## Restaurant mode

- Tap a table at the top of the register (or **Takeaway**). Enter the number of guests if asked.
- Products with options (spice level, extras) open an options dialog; add a kitchen note and course.
- **Send to kitchen** sends new items to the kitchen display. The table's bill is saved on the server, so another device can continue it.
- **Pay** closes the table.
- The kitchen uses **Kitchen display**: Start → Ready → Bump. Tickets older than 15 minutes turn red. **Recall** brings back the last bumped ticket.

## Returns and refunds

Open **Sales**, find the receipt, press **Refund**, choose quantities, the refund method and a reason. Tick *Return items to stock* if they can be resold. A sale can be refunded in parts; the system never refunds more than was sold.

**Void** cancels a whole sale (e.g. rung up by mistake) and reverses stock; it needs the *Void* permission.

## Closing the day

Go to **Shifts**, count the drawer by denomination, and press **Close shift**. The screen shows expected cash, counted cash and the variance.
